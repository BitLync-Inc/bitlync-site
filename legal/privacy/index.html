BITLYNC PRIVACY POLICY

Version 1.0 · Effective on the date of first publication on the Websites

This Privacy Policy is issued by Bitlync, Inc., a Delaware registered corporation with offices located at c/o Legalinc Corporate Services Inc., 131 Continental Dr, Suite 305, Newark, DE 19713 ("Bitlync", "we", "us" or "our"). It describes how we collect, use, disclose and protect personal information when you visit our websites, use the public sandbox, use the Bitlync MSP Portal or ISV console, or otherwise interact with us. Please read it carefully. If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, you should not use the Service.

Interpretation:

1. The definitions and rules of interpretation in this paragraph apply in this Privacy Policy.

Connected System: means a professional services automation, remote monitoring and management, documentation, distribution or other business system which an MSP connects to the Service.

Connection: means the link established between a Connected System and the Service by means of a Credential.

Credential: means any API key, username, password, token, client identifier, client secret or similar secret which an MSP enters into the Service for the purpose of establishing a Connection.

Data Protection Laws: means all Applicable Laws relating to the processing of personal information, including (to the extent applicable) the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act, other United States state privacy laws, the EU General Data Protection Regulation 2016/679 ("GDPR") and the UK General Data Protection Regulation ("UK GDPR").

MSP Data: means all data retrieved from a Connected System through a Connection, all data entered into the Service by an MSP, and all data written to a Connected System through the Service, but excluding Credentials and Usage Data.

Usage Data: means logs, metrics, telemetry and audit records generated by the operation of the Service, including the Audit Log.

2. OUR TWO ROLES

2.1 Bitlync operates a platform which sits between MSPs and ISVs. MSPs connect their business systems to the Service, and ISVs approved by the MSP receive scoped access to the MSP's data. In consequence, Bitlync processes Personal Information in two distinct capacities, and this Privacy Policy is organized accordingly:

(a) As a business and controller. In respect of Personal Information relating to the personnel of our customers, visitors to the Websites, prospective customers and sandbox users, Bitlync determines the purposes and means of processing and is a "business" or "controller" under Data Protection Laws. Paragraphs 3 to 12 apply to such processing.

(b) As a service provider and processor. In respect of Personal Information contained in MSP Data, Bitlync processes such information on behalf of and on the documented instructions of the MSP, and is a "service provider" or "processor" under Data Protection Laws. Paragraph 4 applies to such processing.

2.2 This Privacy Policy does not apply to the products and services of ISVs or of the vendors of Connected Systems, each of which is governed by that organization's own privacy policy.

3. PERSONAL INFORMATION WE COLLECT AS A BUSINESS

3.1 Information you provide to us. We collect: (a) account and contact details, including name, business e-mail address, job title, organization name and address, and telephone number, when you create an MSP Portal or ISV console account, sign in by verified e-mail link, submit the Contact us form, request early access or a demo, or otherwise contact us; (b) billing details of ISVs, including billing contact, invoicing address, tax identifiers and payment method (we do not take card details on the Websites; where an ISV pays by card, the card is entered on a page hosted by our payment processor and we do not store full card numbers); (c) the content of e-mails, support requests, feedback and survey responses; and (d) in the case of ISVs, integration configuration including requested permissions, webhook endpoints and company mappings.

3.2 Information collected automatically. We collect: (a) Usage Data, including records of API calls, writes, logins, Grant approvals, narrowings and revocations, connection health events and console activity, together with the user identity, IP address, timestamp and user agent associated with each event, which Bitlync retains in the Audit Log as a core feature of the Service; (b) ordinary web server logs when you load the Websites, which are hosted on GitHub Pages, including IP address, browser type, operating system, referring page, pages requested and timestamps; and (c) requests made to the public sandbox, including sandbox API keys issued. We do not use advertising pixels. Cookies are described in our Cookie Policy.

3.3 Information from other sources. We collect: (a) from ISVs, the organization name of an MSP and the business e-mail address of the MSP user who will complete a Connection, when the ISV initiates a co-branded Connect flow; and (b) from business partners, events and public professional profiles, the business contact details of prospective customers.

4. MSP DATA: BITLYNC AS SERVICE PROVIDER AND PROCESSOR

4.1 When an MSP establishes a Connection, Bitlync synchronizes data from the Connected System into a per-tenant sync store and serves it to the ISVs which the MSP has approved. MSP Data commonly comprises tickets, companies, contacts, devices, agreements, time entries and installed applications, and accordingly contains Personal Information relating to the MSP's personnel and to the personnel of the MSP's own clients, including names, business contact details, ticket contents and device assignments.

4.2 In respect of MSP Data, the MSP is the business or controller and Bitlync is its service provider or processor. Bitlync processes MSP Data only: (a) to synchronize it from the Connected System and serve it to ISVs within the scope of the Grants approved by the MSP; (b) to deliver writes requested by an ISV within its Grant back to the Connected System; (c) to operate the AI assistant, where enabled, which is read-only and confined to the MSP's tenant; (d) to maintain the Audit Log, connection health and the security of the Service; (e) as otherwise instructed by the MSP or required by Applicable Laws; and (f) to provide ISV Analytics as defined in the MSP Connect Terms.

4.3 Bitlync shall not: (a) sell MSP Data; (b) share MSP Data for cross-context behavioral advertising; (c) use MSP Data to train any generally available machine learning model; (d) aggregate MSP Data across MSPs for publication or sale; (e) share MSP names with vendors or ISVs for the purpose of building a marketplace; (f) create customers in an MSP's professional services automation system; or (g) synchronize documentation passwords or credential objects held in a Connected System. The terms on which Bitlync processes MSP Data are set out in the MSP Connect Terms and the DPA.

4.4 Credentials entered by an MSP are stored in Bitlync's vault, encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM with per-tenant keys, are used solely to authenticate to the Connected System on the MSP's behalf, and are never disclosed to any ISV.

4.5 If you are a client of an MSP and have a question about your Personal Information, you should contact that MSP, which controls the data. Bitlync will refer any request it receives directly to the relevant MSP and will assist the MSP in responding.

5. HOW WE USE PERSONAL INFORMATION AS A BUSINESS

5.1 We use the Personal Information described in paragraph 3 for the following purposes: (a) to provide, operate and secure the Service, including authenticating users, enforcing tenant isolation and rate limits, and maintaining the Audit Log; (b) to show MSP users the permissions each ISV requests when they connect their systems, and to record their acceptances, approvals, narrowings and revocations; (c) to calculate fees, invoice and collect payment from ISVs; (d) to provide support, respond to enquiries and communicate service, security and legal notices; (e) to improve and develop the Service, including through analysis of aggregated or de-identified Usage Data; (f) to send marketing communications about Bitlync products and events, subject to your right to opt out; (g) to detect, investigate and prevent fraud, abuse, security incidents and breaches of our terms; (h) to comply with legal obligations and to establish, exercise or defend legal claims; and (i) for any other purpose disclosed to you at the time of collection or with your consent.

8.1 We implement and maintain administrative, technical and physical safeguards designed to protect Personal Information against accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure or access, including encryption in transit and at rest, per-tenant encryption of Credentials, structural tenant isolation at the point of authorization, role-based access controls, read-only support access, the Audit Log and vulnerability management. Our SOC 2 examination is in progress; we do not claim a completed SOC 2 report or any other certification.

8.3 If we confirm a breach of security affecting Personal Information we hold, we will notify the affected MSP or ISV without undue delay and, where Applicable Laws so require, the affected individuals and regulators, within the periods prescribed by Applicable Laws and the DPA.
