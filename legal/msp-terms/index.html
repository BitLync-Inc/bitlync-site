4. CREDENTIALS AND THE VAULT

4.1 When the MSP establishes a Connection, the MSP shall enter the Credential directly into the Connect Flow. Bitlync shall store the Credential in the Vault, encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM with encryption keys maintained on a per-tenant basis. Bitlync shall act as custodian of the Credential solely for the purpose of operating the Connections and Grants which the MSP has approved.

4.2 No ISV shall receive, view or hold any Credential. Each ISV shall receive only a Grant, being a scoped entitlement on the Connection, and shall access MSP Data through Bitlync's API using an API key issued by Bitlync to that ISV. Bitlync shall not disclose any Credential to any ISV or other third party, save: (a) to the vendor of the applicable Connected System in the ordinary course of authenticating to that system on the MSP's behalf; or (b) where required by Applicable Laws. Bitlync shall not infer, derive or reconstruct any Credential belonging to one MSP or tenant from any Credential belonging to another.

4.3 The Connect Flow shall test each Credential and shall identify, in plain language, any permission required by the Service which is not available on that Credential before the Connection becomes live. A Connection with missing permissions may become live in a reduced state, and the Service shall show the MSP which capabilities are and are not available. Bitlync recommends that the MSP create a dedicated integration user or API member in the Connected System with the minimum permissions required.

4.4 The MSP shall: (a) ensure that each Credential is accurate, valid and one which the MSP is entitled to use; (b) rotate any Credential which it believes to have been compromised, and revoke it in the Connected System where appropriate; (c) keep under its own control all passwords for its Connected Systems which are not entered into the Service; and (d) ensure that only authorized personnel have access to the e-mail mailbox associated with the MSP's account, since the MSP Portal authenticates users by verified e-mail sign-in (magic link).

4.5 The MSP may rotate or delete a Credential at any time in the MSP Portal. Deletion of a Credential shall terminate the Connection and all Grants made on it.

4.6 Bitlync shall not synchronize into the sync store, and shall not make available to any ISV, any documentation password or credential object held in a Connected System (including password entries held in any documentation system).

5. GRANTS TO ISVS

5.1 Each ISV's access to MSP Data shall be the subject of a separate Grant. Before any Grant becomes live, Bitlync shall present to the MSP a Permission Summary identifying the ISV and listing every permission requested by that ISV, showing for each permission whether it is a read or a write, its default state in accordance with paragraph 5.5, and whether the ISV has marked it as Required. No ISV shall receive any access to MSP Data unless and until the MSP has reviewed and approved the applicable Permission Summary.

5.2 Before a Grant becomes live, Bitlync shall test each permission listed in the Permission Summary against the applicable Connection. Where any permission cannot be satisfied, Bitlync shall inform the MSP which permission is affected, and the Grant shall become live only in respect of those permissions which can be satisfied and which the MSP has approved.

5.3 The MSP may narrow or revoke any Grant at any time in the MSP Portal. Revocation shall take effect promptly, whereupon Bitlync shall cease to serve MSP Data to the relevant ISV under that Grant. Revocation of one ISV's Grant shall not affect the Credential, the Connection or any other ISV's Grant. Where an ISV's own agreement with Bitlync terminates, that ISV's Grants shall terminate automatically and the MSP's Connection and any other Grants shall continue unaffected. Revocation shall not require the deletion of MSP Data already received by an ISV, which shall be governed by the applicable ISV Agreement.

5.4 An ISV may write to a Connected System only within the write permissions approved by the MSP on the applicable Permission Summary. Bitlync shall deliver writes using a preview-then-commit model and, where supported by the Connected System, idempotency keys, so that a retried write does not create a duplicate record.

5.5 The MSP alone determines which write permissions are enabled for each ISV, whether on the Permission Summary at the time of approval or later in the MSP Portal, and the write permissions available through the Service are categorized as follows: (a) ordinary writes, being the creation and updating of tickets, the creation of notes, the closing of tickets, the creation of time entries, and billing quantity updates, where the ISV has enabled billing and the MSP has not opted out, subject to any review the MSP sets, are enabled by default; (b) closing a ticket with a resolution (close-with-resolution) is disabled by default; and (c) the updating of companies and agreements, the creation or updating of contacts, projects and custom fields, and device and documentation changes, are disabled unless and until the MSP expressly enables them. An ISV may mark a permission as Required for its integration, in which case that permission starts enabled, but the MSP may nevertheless switch it off, subject to a warning that the ISV’s product may not function correctly. Notwithstanding any Grant or any permission enabled by the MSP, the following operations shall never be performed through the Service: (i) the deletion of records in a Connected System; and (ii) the synchronization or disclosure of any documentation password or credential object, as further provided in paragraph 4.6. A billing write is limited to updating the quantity on a billing record which the MSP has matched to that ISV. The Service currently does not create companies or customers, agreements or contracts, or billing records in the MSP's professional services automation system. Any additional write types will be available only with the MSP's explicit authorization.

5.6 The Service maps only to companies, agreements and billing records which already exist in the MSP's systems. A company created at a distributor, where that write exists and the MSP has enabled it, is treated as any other enabled write under paragraph 5.5.

5.7 Bitlync shall maintain the Audit Log and shall make it available to the MSP in the MSP Portal so that the MSP may see what each ISV has accessed. Bitlync's support personnel may read the Audit Log for the purpose of diagnosing an issue but shall not be able to change the MSP's configuration, act as the MSP in any Connected System or use any Credential.

5.8 Each ISV has agreed with Bitlync to access MSP Data only within the scope of its Grant, to honor any narrowing or revocation, and not to request, solicit or attempt to obtain any Credential. Bitlync shall take reasonable steps to enforce those obligations, including by suspending an ISV's API access, but Bitlync shall not be liable to the MSP for any breach by an ISV of the applicable ISV Agreement.
