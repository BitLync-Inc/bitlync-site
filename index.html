The product

How Bitlync works

One canonical model underneath, two very different experiences on top — boring plumbing for your product, a real connect experience for the MSP.

01 — Your product integrates once

One schema, every vendor

Write against a canonical model — tickets, companies, devices — and every PSA, RMM, and distributor resolves underneath it. No per-vendor branches in your codebase.

02 — Customers connect themselves

Self-serve, guided, validated

Your MSP customers connect their own stack in a few clicks — no engineering time from you, no docs page they have to wire up by hand.

03 — Act through the stack

Read and write, safely

Open a ticket from an alert, update a device, provision a new company — every write takes dry_run and an idempotency_key, so it's previewable first and safe to retry.

Everything that comes with an integration

Every layer you'd otherwise build, bundled

These aren't add-ons. Every layer you'd otherwise build and maintain per vendor comes bundled with every connection.

Caching

Reads are served from our synced store — fast, and free of the vendor's rate budget. Every response tells you where it came from and when it was synced.

Rate-limit handling

We pace every call against each vendor's real limits so your product never trips them by accident.

Retries

Transient vendor failures are retried with backoff. You see one clean result, not a flaky upstream.

Honest delivery status

De-duped + retried — never a silent drop. An acknowledged write can’t double-write; a throttled one gets a real Retry-After.

De-duplication

Idempotency keys on every write mean a retry can never double-create a ticket or a company.

Validation

Payloads are checked against each vendor's rules before they leave — errors surface in your request, not in an MSP's PSA.

Credential vault

MSP credentials live in a per-tenant encrypted vault on our infrastructure. Your product never sees or stores a key.

Health + incidents

Live status per connection, vendor health monitored around the clock, incidents surfaced to you before a partner calls.

Secured by Bitlyncyour credentials are held here — your vendor never sees them

Grants, not shared credentials

You own the connection. Vendors get grants.

You connect your PSA or RMM to Bitlync once — that connection is yours. Each vendor then receives a separate, scoped grant against it. Your credential is entered on our infrastructure, held in an isolated per-tenant vault, and the vendor never touches it. Revoke a grant and that vendor is gone; your connection stays yours.

What a vendor can touch

You see the scope before you connect

Every grant is shown in plain language — including what the vendor can never do:

Readservice tickets, companies, configurations
Writecreate tickets on a board you approve
Neverdelete records · read financials · change your configuration

Our data posture

Plainly stated

Synced once per tenant

One governed sync serves every vendor you've granted. Five tools never mean five pollers spending your shared API rate budget.

Encrypted per-tenant

AES-256-GCM envelope encryption with per-tenant keys. Your tenant is isolated from every other.

Owned by you

Every grant is revocable in one click. Your data is deletable on request.

Audited, append-only

Every read and write by every vendor lands in an append-only log — it can't be edited, and you can see it.

Support that can't touch your data

The support assistant is read-only by construction — it has no write tool. It can diagnose your setup; it cannot change it.

Never resold

Your operational data is not a product. Not aggregated, not sold. Ever.

Architected to SOC 2 controls — certification is on the roadmap
